# COMISET sequence audit ? verified results

**Finding:** The reported counts match the saved sequence records. Very large raw lengths are dominated by repeated labels; these are process telemetry histories, not verified attack campaigns.

## Dataset coverage

Full scan reported. The audit records no malformed JSON, duplicate removals, missing grouping identifiers, or timestamp exclusions (absent counters mean zero). Only labeled events passing the existing 2020?2025 timestamp filter are represented.

## What sequence length means

- **Raw events:** retained event records for one host + process GUID, including repeated labels.
- **Collapsed steps:** consecutive identical label sets reduced to one step. A ? A ? B ? A becomes A ? B ? A.
- **Unique techniques:** distinct technique IDs anywhere in a sequence; order and repetitions do not affect this count.

No inactivity splitting was used. Processes on different hosts or with different GUIDs remain separate. Labels are rule assignments and are not independently validated attack ground truth.
