## Reading of the result (written by hand after the results, 2026-11-28)

1. **Calibration failed.** 0 of 144 committed readings came within 5 points of Fig. 10 (best RMSE 13.29; median
   27.2; a constant guess 20.03). No reading was added afterwards (Section 4, step 4).
2. **Where it fails.** Fig. 10's shape needs one-decoy values of web 68, pad 25, host 1 31, host 2 34, host 3 11,
   file 17 (F3). The selected reading gives 72, 47, 41, 18, 19, 22. Pad and host 1 are too attractive, and
   hosts 2 and 3 are equal. The paper's host 2 vs host 3 gap (F8) cannot come from any reading in the family,
   because the two hosts are identical in Table 2 and Fig. 8. It needs an unpublished, node-specific
   interestingness, which Section 4 ruled out as a free fit.
3. **Primary targets.**
   - Q-learning at 30000 steps: ours 79.20 [78.60, 79.81] vs 80.36. The number is close, but it is
     UNINFORMATIVE: in our environment the best static policy (policy 1, 81.6) is already within 2 points.
   - The claim "Q-learning beats the best static policy by 4.46 points": ours **-2.44 [-2.96, -1.92]**, NO MATCH.
     Here Q-learning does not beat the best fixed deployment, and following-alarm (83.3 on average) beats both.
4. **Following-alarm.** Ours is 85.6 to 80.8 and falls only a little as FNR/FPR rise; the paper's is 72.5 to
   48.7. With this attacker and the alarm model of D9, following the alarms is far stronger than published.
5. **Random-dynamic.** Reading (b) gives 60.7 vs 51.14 (NO MATCH). Reading (a), a sensitivity only, gives 53.3.
6. **Step limit (D8).** In the selected reading, 9.6% of experiments reach the 20-step limit: the attacker keeps
   failing low-probability root exploits (for example web server to data server, 0.04). The limit was fixed
   before the run and is not changed. 21 of 144 readings have more than 5% of experiments at the limit.

**Verdict:** Wang et al. 2020 is **not calibrated-replicated**. The paper's numbers depend on an attacker it does
not publish, and no attacker the text allows (144 readings) reproduces its static-policy figure. As with Li 2025,
its Q-learner can enter a TEMARL comparison only as an adapted comparator inside our environment, under a
frozen comparison protocol.
