The headline

The system works. The architecture claim does not.

✓ What we proved

The deception system contains attackers 60.4% of the time, versus 54.8% for a defender with no attacker model (p = 0.0004).

The Transformer performs statistically identically to GRU and LSTM — using 44% and 35% of their parameters.

✗ What we could not prove

That the Transformer beats GRU or LSTM at reading attacker behaviour.

Tested 8 pre-registered ways, plus a powered replication at n = 41. Every result null. We report this honestly.

The critical discovery

Our first experiment could not have answered its own question

We built a reference ladder to check whether the task could reward sequence modelling at all. The key measure:

Sequence value = (perfect next-step prediction) − (perfect campaign label).
If this is zero, no sequence model can distinguish itself — however good it is.

EnvironmentSequence valueModels beating a no-model baselineEpisode length
Original (experiment v4)+0.00051 of 65.5 steps
Repaired (experiment v5)+0.08005 of 518.1 steps

Cause: the campaigns had been clustered by their own correct answer, and the transition matrix had a bug that discarded 87% of the sequence information. Only the second row is a fair test.

Scientific rigour

We gave the one promising lead a proper second chance

One of the eight tests nearly reached significance. Rather than claim it, we calculated the required sample size, pre-registered a replication at n = 31, ran it once on fresh seeds, and reported the outcome.

Four times a promising small-sample result vanished when tested at proper sample size. Seed-to-seed variation (SD ≈ 0.068) was larger than every effect ever claimed from small samples. This is itself a reportable finding.

8
Diagnosis

Why the architecture claim failed — five reasons

#ReasonEvidence
1The dataset is tiny. Transformers need large data; this has 7 campaigns.Effective n = 3.09
2The sequences are short. Attention's advantage comes from long-range dependencies that do not exist at 18 steps.18.4 steps
3Clustering removed the sequence problem, turning it into 3-way classification.+0.0001
4An estimator bug discarded 87% of the sequential structure in the data.0.627 of 4.83 bits
5The task is nearly saturated. Both models already capture 62–77% of the maximum, leaving less headroom than random noise.~0.02 remaining

Important: reasons 1, 2 and 5 are properties of the problem, not defects in the Transformer. The model is not broken — the task does not contain enough sequential structure for attention to demonstrate an advantage.

9
Contributions

What this thesis contributes

1 · A working system

First Transformer-enhanced MARL honeypot deception system grounded in MITRE D3FEND with CAM-LDS attacker profiles. Captures 62–77% of the maximum achievable gain. Attacker-intent modelling is worth +0.148 to +0.171 containment.

2 · Proven parameter efficiency

Statistically demonstrated equivalence to GRU and LSTM at 44.4% and 34.7% of their parameters. Not a null result — a positive equivalence claim, p = 0.0001.

3 · A methodological finding

A pre-registered environment gate can pass while the environment is useless for the hypothesis being tested. Architecture comparisons need a reference ladder and a validity gate — without them, v4 produced 8 null results from 5 models that had never learned.

4 · Data contributions

A source-verified CAM-LDS grounding with 5 parser defects found and fixed, verified independently; a quantified estimator defect that destroyed 87% of the corpus's sequential information.

11
Conclusion

The honest version of this thesis is the defensible one

The title's implied claim — that attention is what makes this system work — is not supported. What is supported is more precise:

A Transformer-based honeypot deception system that captures 62–77% of the achievable defensive gain, and matches recurrent alternatives using roughly one-third of their parameters — established under pre-registration, on an instrument we first had to prove was capable of measuring it.

Limitation

Trained by imitation learning, not reinforcement learning — under PPO no model beat the baseline, and why remains open.

Limitation

One environment, one scripted attacker. A learning attacker could change the conclusions.

Future work

Test entity attention on genuinely varying topologies with more power; resolve the PPO failure.
